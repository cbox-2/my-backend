How many users can I have online?

NexusBox is engineered to support very high concurrency. On our paid plans you can have hundreds of users online at once. If you have a large platform with tens of thousands of concurrent users or more, we offer the Pro+ plan, which allows you to select the capacity you require.

Do you have a demo?

The NexusBox on the home page is a live example of NexusBox Pro. Instead of a demo plan, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on your purchase.

Does NexusBox work on mobile?

Yes, NexusBox supports modern smartphones with its responsive and lightweight design. We provide you with a direct link for your NexusBox which is ideal for mobile access, but if your site layout is responsive, then NexusBox will work well embedded, too.

Can I change the look & feel?

Customization is a feature with NexusBox: we have over 50 configurable settings, over 30 language localizations, and you can customize the colours, fonts and CSS of your NexusBox to make it seamless with your site. Our paid plans are also free of branding.
