Problems installing?

If you see code on your page instead of your NexusBox, your editor may be stripping or sanitizing NexusBox HTML.

If your platform supports apps or widgets, you may be able to find one that allows you to embed your NexusBox. Search for an "iframe" or "embed" app or widget.

If the app or widget requires a URL, enter http://34.61.70.211/public/box/?boxid=1000001&boxtag=abc123.
